ASSET DIMENSIONS & FORMATS — Speak Frankly
==========================================
Verified against the actual files in this folder. Every number below was read
out of the PNG's own IHDR header, not assumed.

Checked on: build 1.5.3 (versionCode 23)
Source device for screenshots: Samsung Galaxy A34 5G (SM-A346E), 1080x2340, Android 16


GOOGLE PLAY REQUIREMENTS
------------------------
App icon
  512 x 512 px
  32-bit PNG (alpha channel present)
  Max 1 MB
  Play applies its own rounded/adaptive mask — do not pre-round the corners

Feature graphic
  1024 x 500 px
  JPEG or 24-bit PNG — NO alpha channel
  Max 15 MB
  Gets cropped and overlaid differently across Play surfaces, so keep meaning
  inside a safe margin

Phone screenshots
  2 to 8 required (we ship 8, the maximum)
  JPEG or 24-bit PNG — NO alpha channel
  Min 320 px on any side; max 3840 px on any side
  The long side may not exceed 2x the short side
  Max 8 MB each


WHAT THIS FOLDER SHIPS

icon/icon-512.png

HOW TO REGENERATE
-----------------
  node scripts/render.mjs              # both feature graphics
  node scripts/render.mjs feature      # just the main one
  node scripts/capture.mjs             # list device + remaining screenshots
  node scripts/build-screenshots.mjs   # composite raw captures into finals

render.mjs re-reads each PNG's header after writing it and fails loudly if the
size is even one pixel off, because Play rejects the upload rather than
resizing it for you.
