## 3. Full description  — 2631/4000 characters

```
You know the words. You still freeze when it is time to speak.

Speak Frankly is built for that moment — the interview, the shop counter, the call you keep putting off. You practise by actually talking, with an AI partner that never sighs, never rushes you, and never makes you feel small.

TALK YOUR WAY THROUGH REAL SITUATIONS
Order food. Sit a job interview. Shop for clothes. Each scenario is a real conversation, not a quiz. Filter by level (A1, A2, B1) or search for the one you need.

TALK ABOUT ANYTHING
Type any topic at all and the tutor builds a conversation around it, so you can rehearse the exact situation you are worried about.

A TUTOR THAT MATCHES YOUR LEVEL
Say it simply and it answers simply. Improve and it stretches you. It replies to what you MEANT, even when the grammar came out wrong, then shows you one or two fixes — no red pen, no lecture.

NEVER STUCK STARING AT AN EMPTY BOX
At beginner levels you get ready-to-tap replies every turn. One tap and the conversation keeps moving. That is how you build the habit of answering instead of freezing.

SHADOWING PRACTICE
Listen to a phrase, repeat it out loud, and see a word-by-word breakdown of how much came through clearly. It measures how well you were understood, so you can hear yourself getting better.

STORY MODE
Guided role-plays where you pick your reply and the story branches. Works offline.

TAP ANY WORD YOU DO NOT KNOW
Meaning, pronunciation and audio, plus a translation in your own language — without leaving the conversation. Bookmark the words worth keeping and they land in Saved Words for review.

WORD OF THE DAY
A useful word every day, with a simple meaning and an example sentence you can actually use.

PRACTICE THAT DOES NOT FEEL LIKE HOMEWORK
Picture match, word games and fill-in-the-blanks, plus spaced-repetition review that brings words back just before you would forget them.

SEE YOURSELF IMPROVING
A fluency map tracks conversations, speaking, vocabulary and consistency. Streaks, XP and badges keep you coming back.

BUILT AROUND YOU
A short placement test finds your level. Onboarding asks your language, your goal, and what you struggle with most. The interface is available in English, Hindi, Spanish, French and Portuguese.

PRACTISE ANYWHERE
Download scenarios for offline practice and set a daily reminder. Sign in with Google and your progress follows you to any device.

FREE TO START
Begin with a free trial, then keep practising every day on the free plan. Premium removes ads and unlocks unlimited conversation.

Ten minutes of talking beats an hour of reading grammar rules. Open the app and say something.
```

## 6. BEFORE publishing — two declarations that can get this rejected

**Data safety** (Policy → App content → Data safety):

- [ ] Declare the **advertising ID**. The app serves real AdMob banner,
      interstitial and rewarded ads, so it collects one. Failing to declare this
      is a common rejection reason.
- [ ] Add the **account deletion URL**: `https://speak-frankly.onrender.com/delete-account`
      Play requires a deletion route for any app with accounts. In-app deletion
      now also exists at Profile → Delete account.

**Then:** preview the listing exactly as a user sees it, at phone width.

> If Play shows ANY warning, policy flag or missing declaration — stop there and
> ask, rather than clicking "Send for review".
