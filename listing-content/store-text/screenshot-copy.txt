SCREENSHOT HEADLINE COPY — Speak Frankly
========================================

Eight phone screenshots, in listing order. Headline is the large line; support
is the smaller line beneath it. Every claim maps to a feature that exists in
build 1.5.3 (versionCode 23) and was verified on a real device.

Rules applied:
  - No "Download now" / "Install now" (Play discourages install CTAs in shots)
  - No ratings, review counts, awards, user numbers or superlatives
  - No Google Play badges
  - Headlines <= 42 characters so they stay readable at thumbnail size

FEATURE / BENEFIT BULLETS (reusable for the description or a website)
  - Talk through real situations: ordering food, job interviews, shopping
  - Type any topic and the tutor builds a conversation around it
  - Replies matched to your level, with at most two gentle corrections a turn
  - Ready-to-tap replies so beginners are never stuck on an empty text box
  - Shadowing practice with a word-by-word breakdown of what was understood
  - Story mode role-plays that branch on your choice, and work offline
  - Tap any word for meaning, audio and a translation in your language
  - Saved Words plus spaced-repetition review
  - Picture match and word games
  - Fluency map, streaks, XP and badges
  - Placement test to find your level
  - Interface in English, Hindi, Spanish, French and Portuguese
