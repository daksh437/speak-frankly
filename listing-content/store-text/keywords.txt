SUGGESTED KEYWORDS — Speak Frankly
==================================

IMPORTANT: Google Play has NO keywords field. Unlike the App Store, Play indexes
your title, short description and full description. So these terms are not
pasted anywhere — they are the vocabulary the copy should already contain
naturally. Every term below appears in the current description in a real
sentence. Do not add a keyword list to the listing itself: Google Play's
metadata policy treats keyword stuffing as a violation and it can get the
listing rejected or demoted.

INTENTIONALLY NOT USED
  "best english app", "no.1 english app", "#1"     — unsupported superlatives
  "fluent in 30 days", "guaranteed fluency"        — outcome claims we cannot back
  "free english course", "unlimited free"          — misleading; the free tier is capped
  "IELTS", "TOEFL", "exam preparation"             — the app has no exam content
  "native speaker teacher", "certified teacher"    — there are no human teachers
  "accent training", "accent reduction"            — scoring measures intelligibility,
                                                     not accent (see README)
