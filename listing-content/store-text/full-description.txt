Speak Frankly is an AI English tutor you practise by TALKING to — not by memorising grammar rules. Real conversations, gentle corrections, and a partner that never sighs, never rushes you, and never makes you feel small.

🗣️ TALK TO AN AI TUTOR
Order food. Sit a job interview. Shop for clothes. See a doctor. Ask for directions. Check into a hotel. Take a work phone call. Each one is a real conversation, not a quiz — and the tutor answers what you MEANT, even when your grammar came out wrong.

✏️ CORRECTIONS THAT DON'T STING
At most one or two fixes a turn, with a one-line reason you can actually use. Say "I am working here since two years" and it shows you "I have worked here for two years — use 'for' with a period of time." No red pen. No lecture.

💬 NEVER STUCK ON AN EMPTY TEXT BOX
Beginners get ready-to-tap replies every single turn. One tap and the conversation keeps moving. That is how you build the habit of answering instead of freezing.

🎯 TALK ABOUT ANYTHING
Type any topic at all — cricket, your job, your visa interview, your favourite film — and the tutor builds a conversation around it, at your level. Rehearse the exact situation you are dreading.

🎙️ SPEAK OUT LOUD
Shadowing practice: listen to a phrase, say it back, and see word by word how much of it came through clearly. You can also speak your replies in any conversation instead of typing them.

📖 STORY MODE — WORKS OFFLINE
Guided role-plays where you pick your reply and the story branches. A restaurant, a clinic, a hotel front desk. No internet needed, so you can practise on the train, on a flight, or anywhere the signal drops.

📚 TAP ANY WORD YOU DON'T KNOW
Meaning, phonetic spelling, audio, and a translation in your own language — without leaving the conversation. Bookmark the ones worth keeping and they land in Saved Words.

🔁 REVIEW SO IT ACTUALLY STICKS
Spaced repetition brings saved words back just before you would have forgotten them. Practise them with matching, fill-in-the-blank and word-guess games.

🖼️ PRACTICE THAT DOESN'T FEEL LIKE HOMEWORK
Picture match, daily speaking challenges and a Word of the Day with a meaning and an example sentence you can use today. All matched to your level.

🔥 SEE YOURSELF IMPROVING
A fluency map tracks four things that matter: conversations, speaking, vocabulary and consistency. Daily streaks, XP and eight badges to unlock keep you coming back — and new scenarios unlock as you earn XP.
