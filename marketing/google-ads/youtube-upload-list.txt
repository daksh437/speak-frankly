GOOGLE ADS — VIDEO ASSETS TO UPLOAD
===================================

App campaigns cannot take an MP4 directly. Each video has to be on YouTube
first (PUBLIC or UNLISTED — private videos cannot be used in ads), then you
paste the watch URL into the campaign.

Google recommends 10-30 seconds for app campaigns, so the 45s cuts are left out
below. They exist if you want them later, but they are outside the recommended
range and tend to lose viewers before the message lands.

The slot holds 20. This is 18, ordered so that if you stop early you still have
a usable spread.


ROUND 1 — all five messages, all three shapes (15 files)
--------------------------------------------------------
Aspect ratio matters most: Google serves different placements (YouTube feed,
in-stream, Discover, Play) and a missing shape simply does not compete there.
Message variety matters next — five different angles is what lets the algorithm
find out which one works. Do not upload five versions of one idea.

  concept-01  problem / solution      15s-16x9.mp4  15s-1x1.mp4  15s-9x16.mp4
  concept-02  AI English tutor        15s-16x9.mp4  15s-1x1.mp4  15s-9x16.mp4
  concept-03  fear of mistakes        15s-16x9.mp4  15s-1x1.mp4  15s-9x16.mp4
  concept-04  feature tour            15s-16x9.mp4  15s-1x1.mp4  15s-9x16.mp4
  concept-05  complete experience     15s-16x9.mp4  15s-1x1.mp4  15s-9x16.mp4


ROUND 2 — longer cut of the two strongest angles (3 files)
-----------------------------------------------------------
30s gives the correction moment room to actually read. Pick the two concepts
you believe in most; these two are the suggestion, not a measurement.

  concept-01  30s-9x16.mp4        (portrait carries most app-install volume)
  concept-03  30s-9x16.mp4
  concept-04  30s-16x9.mp4


NOT for ads
-----------
  videos/**/45s-*.mp4
      Outside Google's recommended 10-30s window.

  listing-content/video/speak-frankly-promo.mp4
      43.6s, built for the Play STORE LISTING, not for ads. Different job:
      it assumes someone already clicked through and is deciding.


After uploading
---------------
Name them on YouTube so you can tell them apart in reporting later, e.g.
  "Speak Frankly - 01 problem-solution - 15s 9x16"

Then paste each watch URL into the campaign's Videos section.
